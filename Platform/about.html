Who We Are

Transforming Education. Transforming Humanity.

At Be Human Bring-up Human, we believe education should nurture academic and holistic excellence through a humanistic approach. We empower learners, teachers and parents to create learning environments that foster confidence, empathy, resilience, values and respect, nurturing capable minds, compassionate hearts and better human beings.

Our Journey

The idea and dream of building an Education Solutions enterprise was envisioned by Founder, Priyanka G, in 2022. What began as a personal vision gradually evolved through years of learning, experience, and insights gained from the field of education.

The idea was founded on October 8, 2022, and took a significant step forward with its official registration on September 2, 2026.

More than building an organisation, BHBH represents years of learning, ideas, experience, and a deep belief in meaningful education—brought to life with purpose.

Our Vision

"To create a world where education goes beyond knowledge and skills to nurture dignity, empathy, compassion, inclusion, responsibility and meaningful human connection: empowering every individual to learn, grow and contribute to a peaceful and happy world."

"Making learning simpler, meaningful and effective: empowering students to understand deeply, think creatively, apply knowledge and create solutions."

Our Philosophy

"What then is the purpose of national education? Rather than devise complex theoretical interpretations, it is better to start by looking to the lovely child who sits on your knee and ask yourself: What can I do to assure that this child will be able to lead the happiest life possible?"

— Tsunesaburo Makiguchi

Education is not just about what a child learns, but about who the child becomes. When teachers, parents and educators learn to approach children with empathy, dignity and understanding, they create the conditions for children to grow into confident, compassionate and responsible human beings.

Our Approach

INFORM

Building awareness, understanding and knowledge.

EMPOWER

Helping individuals recognise their voice, choices, strengths and responsibilities.

EQUIP

Providing practical skills, strategies and tools that can be applied in real situations.

HUMANISE

Putting dignity, empathy, compassion and respect at the heart of education and relationships.
