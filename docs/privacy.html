Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

At Be Human Bring-up Human (BHBH), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

Note for parents and children: Our website is designed for adult educators, parents, and school representatives. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you use our contact form or email us. This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Organisation or school name
  • Any messages or enquiry details you choose to share

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries regarding workshops, programmes, and partnerships.
  • To communicate with you about your specific requests.
  • To improve our services and understanding of our audience.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We believe in a privacy-first approach. This website does not use third-party tracking cookies by default. We do not track your activity for advertising purposes. Any analytics tools we may implement in the future will be privacy-friendly and will respect "Do Not Track" browser settings.

4. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We implement appropriate data collection, storage, and processing practices to protect against unauthorized access or disclosure of your personal information.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

BHBH has the discretion to update this privacy policy at any time. We encourage users to frequently check this page for any changes. You acknowledge and agree that it is your responsibility to review this privacy policy periodically.

6. Contacting Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
